Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Lash On Wheels ("we", "us", or "our") is a mobile marketplace for on-demand beauty services. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Lash On Wheels application.

Information we collect

When you create an account or sign in, we may receive your name, email address, account identifier, and authentication information from the sign-in method you choose, including Google or email and password.

During the current account setup flow, we store your profile name, account role, and requested role so we can provide the appropriate application experience.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and authenticate your account;
  • maintain your profile and role-based access;
  • provide, secure, and improve the application; and
  • respond to support requests.

Service providers

We use Supabase to provide authentication, database, and related infrastructure services. If you choose Google sign-in, Google processes your information under its own privacy policy.

Storage and security

Authentication session information is stored using the app's supported session storage so that you can remain signed in. We use access controls and database policies intended to protect account data. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and deletion

We retain account information while your account is active or as needed to provide the application. To request account or data deletion, contact us using the address below.

Children's privacy

Lash On Wheels is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the application develops. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the date above.
